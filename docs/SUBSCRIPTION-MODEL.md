# Kairon: support subscriptions

Kairon is source-available under the Zyvor Production License v1.0. Non-production use is free; production use requires a commercial license (sales@zyvor.dev).

Teams that want supported releases and defined response targets can buy an annual **support subscription** from Zyvor.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Community | The software, community documentation and public issue tracking | No contracted response target |
| Standard | Deployment and upgrade guidance, defined supported configuration | Business hours |
| Supported | Standard plus priority queue and incident triage | Business hours, faster response |
| Enterprise | Supported plus a named technical contact and 24x7 critical intake | One-hour initial response target for critical issues |

Support is sized per worker node on the one Zyvor Support meter (per node per year; one meter covers all Zyvor infrastructure software on a node, never stacked per product): **Supported INR 59,999 per node per year in India (minimum 5 nodes, INR 299,995) / USD 600 elsewhere (minimum USD 3,000)**; **Supported Plus INR 119,999 per node per year in India (minimum 5 nodes, INR 599,995) / USD 1,200 elsewhere (minimum USD 6,000)**. INR figures include 18% GST; USD figures exclude tax. Minimums are annual contract values. **Supported Plus** adds 24x7 Severity 1 and 2, quarterly reviews and upgrade assistance. Volume: 256-499 nodes -10%, 500-999 -15%, 1,000+ -20%; multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%; volume and multi-year together are capped at 25%. Non-production use of the software stays free under the Zyvor Production License v1.0.

Buy / get a quote: [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy). Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev).

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Rights under the Zyvor Production License v1.0 continue as stated in the license.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
